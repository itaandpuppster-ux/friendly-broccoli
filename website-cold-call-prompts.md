# Cold-Call Web Design Playbook — 5 Ready-to-Use Website Prompts

**Offer:** Custom, first-class website (~10 pages) → **$600 upfront + $200/month for 8 months** (~$2,200/client, first year).
**Use with:** Any AI site builder — v0, Lovable, Bolt, Claude, Cursor, Framer AI, Webflow AI, etc. Paste a prompt, then swap in the real business name, city, and photos.

---

## Why these 5 businesses (easy to reach, easy to say "yes")

| # | Business type | Why they close fast |
|---|---------------|---------------------|
| 1 | **Med Spa / Aesthetics clinic** | One Botox client = ~$400+. $200/mo is a rounding error. Vanity-driven → they want to look premium. |
| 2 | **Landscaping / Lawn Care** | High job value ($2k–$15k), most have an ugly or no site, owners answer their own phones. |
| 3 | **Roofing / Exterior contractor** | One roof = $10k–$30k. A single lead pays for the whole year. Desperate for online credibility. |
| 4 | **Family-owned Italian restaurant** (food) | Owner-operated, proud of the brand, hates their outdated site. Emotional, fast "yes." |
| 5 | **Dental practice** | Recurring high-value patients, marketing budget already exists, cares deeply about trust/appearance. |

**Best first calls:** Roofing and Med Spas (highest customer value → lowest price resistance). Restaurants are the *easiest emotional yes* but thinnest margins — lead with them if you want quick wins.

---

## The 30-second cold-call script (use before you send the prompt's output)

> "Hi, is this the owner? Great — my name's [You]. I build websites for [med spas / roofers / etc.] here in [City], and I noticed [yours is a little dated / you don't have one / it's not mobile-friendly]. I already mocked up a modern version for [Business Name] — can I text or email you the link so you can see it? … If you love it, it's **$600 to launch and $200 a month** for hosting, updates, and support. Fair enough?"

The trick: **build the site first** using the prompts below, then show it on the call. Seeing beats selling.

# PROMPT 1 — Med Spa / Aesthetics Clinic

Copy everything in the box below into your AI website builder.

```
Build a first-class, modern, 10-page website for an upscale medical spa called
"[BUSINESS NAME]" located in [CITY, STATE]. Tone: luxurious, clean, calming,
trustworthy — think a blend of a 5-star wellness resort and a modern clinic.

DESIGN SYSTEM:
- Color palette: soft ivory/cream background, warm nude & blush accents, deep
  espresso or charcoal text, a single elegant gold or sage accent.
- Typography: elegant serif for headings (e.g., Cormorant/Playfair), clean sans
  for body (Inter/Nunito). Generous white space, large hero imagery.
- Feel: airy, high-end, spa-like. Subtle fade/scroll animations, rounded soft
  corners, soft shadows. Fully responsive and fast on mobile.

PAGES (10):
1. Home — full-screen hero, tagline, trust badges, service highlights, before/
   after teaser, testimonials, "Book a Free Consultation" CTA.
2. About — founder story, medical credentials, mission, team photos.
3. Services (overview) — grid of treatment categories with icons.
4. Injectables & Fillers — Botox, dermal fillers, pricing tiers, FAQs.
5. Skin Treatments — facials, chemical peels, microneedling, laser.
6. Body Contouring / Wellness — CoolSculpting, IV therapy, etc.
7. Before & After Gallery — filterable image gallery with results.
8. Pricing & Memberships — transparent packages, monthly membership offer.
9. Testimonials & Reviews — cards, star ratings, Google review embed.
10. Contact & Book — booking widget, map, hours, phone, contact form.

GLOBAL: sticky nav with "Book Now" button, floating call/text button on mobile,
newsletter signup in footer, HIPAA-friendly consultation form, social links,
click-to-call phone number everywhere. Add SEO meta titles/descriptions per page.
Use tasteful placeholder images I can replace. Make every page feel premium.
```

## How to run the whole hustle (quick playbook)

1. **Pick a niche** from the 5 above (roofing or med spas convert fastest).
2. **Find 20 local businesses** on Google Maps with weak/no websites.
3. **Build the demo first** — paste the matching prompt, drop in their real name + a couple of their photos (takes 10–15 min per site).
4. **Cold call / walk in** with the script above and show the live demo.
5. **Close at $600 + $200/mo** — the $200 covers hosting, edits, and support, so it's recurring income.
6. **Deliver, then upsell** — Google Business optimization, SEO, more pages, ads.

**Math:** 1 client = ~$2,200/yr. Ten clients = ~$22,000/yr in largely passive recurring revenue. Land 2–3 a month and it compounds fast.

---

### Tip: make each demo feel "custom"
Before the call, add one hyper-local detail to the prompt — e.g. *"reference [City] and the [local landmark] neighborhood,"* or *"mention they've served [City] since 1998."* That single touch makes the owner feel it was built just for them, which is what turns a maybe into a yes.
