— 01 / The method

Three moves from flat to alive

01

Displace

Every surface is a field of points. Push them with layered waves and a plane becomes an ocean, a dune, a breathing skin — computed live on the GPU.

02

Light it

Warm, physically-grounded lighting reads the geometry as it moves, so shadow and highlight fall exactly where the form bends. Paper-real, never plastic.

03

Respond

The surface follows your cursor. Interaction isn't a feature bolted on top — it's the material itself, reacting frame by frame at sixty per second.
